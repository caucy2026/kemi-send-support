KEMI Send Privacy

Effective September 14, 2026

KEMI Send transfers files, folders, text, and clipboard content directly between devices on the same local network. The Mac App Store version does not require an account.

Data handling

  • Transfer content is sent directly to the device selected by the user and is not uploaded to this support site.
  • The app uses local-network access to discover nearby KEMI Send devices and complete transfers.
  • The app accesses only files and folders the user explicitly selects or accepts as a transfer destination.
  • Basic settings and transfer history may be stored locally on the device.

Support requests

Information a user voluntarily posts in the public support tracker is processed by GitHub under GitHub's own terms and privacy policy. Users should not post passwords, private files, or other sensitive information.
